It's been a little over a month or two since I started development on this website. Now, while I can't exactly say it's **all** done just yet; I do believe that it's in a presentable state. I have some ideas of what else I want to put in this website, though for now, those can wait...

# The XMB aesthetic

I absolutely adore the XMB aesthetic because of how effortlessly sleek it still looks, even after all these years. There’s something genuinely timeless about its design—the clean horizontal layout, subtle animations, minimalist icons, and restrained visual language all work together without ever feeling cluttered or dated. What I like most is that it isn’t just beautiful for the sake of being beautiful; it’s also incredibly functional. Everything feels intuitive, easy to navigate, and visually consistent. 

Even now, the XMB still feels modern in a way that a lot of newer interfaces don’t, and that balance between simplicity, elegance, and usability is what makes the aesthetic so appealing to me.

By and large, I like to think of myself as a pretty utilitarian person. I've always found it difficult to design something that looks genuinely good without getting caught up in every tiny detail, so my old website reflected that pretty heavily. It was simple, functional, and did what I needed it to do—but there wasn't much personality to it.

I think that's part of why I gravitated toward the XMB aesthetic so much. It manages to be extremely functional without sacrificing its identity. It's minimal without feeling empty, and stylish without getting in the way of actually using it. That's more or less the balance I wanted to bring into this version of the website.

# What the website is for

At its core, this website is a place for me to show off the things I've built and, more generally, who I am as a person. I wanted something that went beyond a traditional portfolio where everything is reduced to a project card and a few bullet points. I want this to be a space where I can actually talk about the work I do, the things I'm interested in, and the ideas that occupy my time.

A big part of that will be technical write-ups. I spend a lot of time programming, reverse engineering, experimenting with low-level systems, and generally digging into things just to understand how they work, so I want somewhere to document that process properly. At the same time, I don't want everything here to be strictly technical. I'd also like to post other pieces of writing whenever I have something I feel is worth putting into words.

# What's next?

I still have plenty of ideas for the website, both visually and functionally, and I'm sure it'll continue to change as I use it more. For now, though, I'm happy to finally have a place that feels like my own.
